The U.S. produces more natural gas than any country on earth — about — bcf/d of dry gas, mostly from the Appalachian, Permian, and Haynesville shale plays. The system runs on pipelines, balanced by working-gas storage that injects in summer and withdraws in winter. A decade ago LNG exports were zero; today they're — bcf/d and growing. The EIA storage report drops every Thursday at 10:30 ET.
