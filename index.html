Energy is the most literal plumbing there is — actual pipes, tanks, refineries, and ports. These pages draw the U.S. petroleum system the way it really moves: flow widths scale with barrels-per-day, tank fills show inventory levels, and every node carries its EIA series ID. Click any element of the overview schematic to drill into the sub-topic — by PADD, by source country, by destination, by salt-dome site.

The Plumbing of the U.S. Petroleum Balance

One page. The full crude → refining → product → demand circuit with the Strategic Petroleum Reserve overlaid. Every node is clickable.
Detail · Crude Production

Domestic crude output by PADD

Where the ~13.7 mb/d comes out of the ground. Gulf Coast and the Permian dominate; right rail ranks the top eight producing states.
Detail · Crude Imports

Imports by source country

Canada via pipeline (WCS heavy sour). Mexico, Saudi, Colombia, Iraq. OPEC share has collapsed from ~70% to under 15% since 2008.

Refinery throughput, utilization & yield

132 refineries · 18 mb/d nameplate capacity. Per-PADD utilization bars and the gasoline / distillate / jet / other yield split.
Detail · Crude Exports

Crude exports by destination

Light sweet U.S. shale that Gulf refineries don't want, shipped out of Corpus Christi and Houston to Asia and Europe.
Detail · Gasoline

Gasoline production & stocks

Refiner output by PADD on the left, regional stocks on the right. The product the American driver feels at the pump.
Detail · Distillate

Diesel + heating oil

The cleanest read on industrial demand — freight, farm, NE heating. Distillate margins spike before recessions break.
Detail · Jet & Other

Jet fuel + the long-tail products

Jet, residual fuel oil, LPG, naphtha, asphalt, coke. Everything from the bottom of the barrel that doesn't fit gasoline or distillate.
Detail · Commercial Crude

Working crude inventory by PADD

Tank-by-tank breakdown of the working barrels at refineries and in transit. Cushing called out — it's the WTI delivery hub.
Detail · Strategic Reserve

SPR drawdown across four salt-dome sites

Bryan Mound, West Hackberry, Big Hill, Bayou Choctaw. Tanks sized by capacity; the 2026 supply-shock narrative lives here.
Detail · Product Demand

Product supplied breakdown

The EIA proxy for end-use demand — gasoline 45%, distillate 20%, jet 8%, everything else 27%.
All series sourced from the U.S. Energy Information Administration and the U.S. Department of Energy. Refreshed weekly on Thursdays following the EIA WPSR release (Wed ~10:30 ET). Source: GitHub → SecondOrderEdge/energy-mechanics.
