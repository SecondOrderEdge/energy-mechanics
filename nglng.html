The first U.S. LNG cargo loaded at Sabine Pass in February 2016. A decade later the U.S. is the largest LNG exporter on earth — — bcf/d of feed gas flowing through eight operating terminals on the Gulf and East Coasts. Three more (Plaquemines Phase 2, Rio Grande, Port Arthur) are ramping or under construction. Europe took most of the cargoes after 2022; Asia is the long-run growth.
